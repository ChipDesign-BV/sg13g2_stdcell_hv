# HV latch family: sg13g2_hv_{dllrq,dlhq,dlhr,dllr}_1

Four new DRC-clean / LVS-clean thick-oxide latch layouts, generated by
`gen_latches.py` in this directory.  Gate: `python3 work/cell_verify.py
work/cell_dev/latches/<cell>.gds` from the repo root — all four print
PASS (fresh combined run: `final_verify.out`; per-cell JSON in
`<cell>.verify.json`).

## How they were built (common to all four)

The reference cell sg13g2_hv_dlhrq_1 was itself produced by the library
1-D retarget (`work/layout_retarget.py`) from the thin-oxide
sg13g2_dlhrq_1.  The four missing latches also exist in the thin-oxide
library but were skipped by the retarget for one reason: each connects
one or two PMOS VDD source diffusions to the rail by **butting p+ Activ
straight into the n+ VDD tap** ("PMOS Activ runs up into the VDD rail").
Instead of hand-rewiring dlhrq_1 (its Metal1 plan leaves no legal
corridor for the enable-phase swap without a full reroute), each cell is
generated from its own thin-oxide twin with the proven pilot recipe of
`work/flop_pilot/gen_dfrbpq.py`:

1. remove the butting finger(s) from Activ before the map (exact drawn
   boxes, asserted);
2. run the unmodified library retarget machinery with the **library**
   y-map (band 1.925..3.630 x2.40, inserts rail +0.43 / NW+track +0.445
   / pSD +0.10; these cells' NMOS bands are low, so no custom cut was
   needed): transform, contact re-tile, notch closing, automatic M1.e
   repair, site padding, ThickGateOx + DigiBnd;
3. restore the fingers in the thick-oxide frame from the mapped PMOS
   body top up to y = 6.99, where they merge the tap strip (butted
   p+/n+ junction, sg13g2_hv_slgcp_1 convention; the LVS deck connects
   it through psd_ntap_abutt).  The mapped strap contacts and their VDD
   rail M1 fingers land back on top;
4. rebuild the N-well with the strict construction of
   `work/fix_well_nwc1.py` (lateral overhang exactly ±0.62, bottom
   2.570, NW.c1/d1/f1 square-corner asserts; local jogs below 2.570
   where deep PMOS Activ needs them, kept ≥0.62 from the cell edges);
5. re-tile the rail tap contacts onto the site-centred 0.48 grid with
   the guards of `work/fix_rail_contacts.py`;
6. guarded Metal1 edits for the M1.e sites the automatic repair cannot
   place (library `M1E_EDITS` style: every edit re-checked in code for
   M1.a width, M1.b space and contact enclosure).

Device widths are exact by construction: the slab retarget emits every
PMOS width as snap(2.40 × thin-oxide height) pinned to the mapped slab
top, which reproduces the shipped CDL values (0.42→1.010, 0.84→2.015,
1.00→2.400, 1.12→2.690); NMOS widths are untouched.  LVS golden is the
`.SUBCKT` in `cdl/sg13g2_stdcell_hv.cdl` for each cell.

## Per cell (relative to sg13g2_hv_dlhrq_1, 27 sites)

### sg13g2_hv_dllrq_1 — 29 sites (13.92 µm)
Same 20-device multiset as dlhrq_1; only the enable wiring differs:
pin GATE_N instead of GATE, the internal inverter chain produces gnb =
inv(GATE_N) and gnbb = inv(gnb), and the four pass devices swap phases
(MN3/MP6 gates on gnb, MN5/MP4 gates on gnbb, where dlhrq_1 drives
MN3/MP6 from GATE_BB and MN5/MP4 from GATE_B).  The layout is the
retargeted thin-oxide dllrq_1 (independent drawn placement, not an edit
of dlhrq_1's polygons): device order along the row and all routing
follow the LV cell.  One butted VDD finger (LV x2.695..2.995) removed /
restored.  Width +2 sites vs dlhrq_1 (the LV twin is drawn wider).
No Metal1 edits needed.  Magic reports 4 M1.e (known
magic-vs-klayout interpretation diff, non-fatal per cell_verify; the
KLayout maximal deck is clean).

### sg13g2_hv_dlhq_1 — 30 sites (14.40 µm)
dlhrq_1 minus the reset pair (MN8 0.740 / MP8 2.400 in dlhrq_1 —
netlist-wise −2×0.640 NMOS, −2×2.400 PMOS, +1×0.740 NMOS, +1×2.690
PMOS after the resized MN3/MN4/MP7), no RESET_B pin; 18 devices.
Layout from the LV twin.  Two butted VDD fingers removed/restored.
The LV cell draws one PMOS S/D complex bottom-aligned (bottoms at
1.905, below the band bottom 1.925): the analyse() bottom guard is
overridden (slab mapping stays exact) and the mapped complex
(x7.68..10.67) is translated up by 0.205 µm so the strict-well jog
(bottom 2.435) clears this cell's own NMOS halos; gate endcaps and
contact enclosures asserted.  Metal1 edits: D pin pad narrowed
1.255→1.215 (M1.e vs the column at 1.435), and two 0.02-deep notches
in the contact column at x1.805 split a 1.75 µm parallel run
(sdfbbp_1-precedent run splitting).

### sg13g2_hv_dlhr_1 — 31 sites (14.88 µm)
dlhrq_1 plus the Q_N output inverter (MP10 2.015, MP11 2.690, MN10
0.550, MN11 0.740) and MP7/MP8 resized 2.400→2.690; 24 devices; pins
Q Q_N D GATE RESET_B.  Layout from the LV twin; two butted VDD fingers
removed/restored; no Activ normalisation needed.  Metal1 edits: tab
bottom at (8.26..8.53, 1.91) raised 0.035 (parallel run 1.015→0.98),
Q pin pad narrowed 12.08→12.04 above y4.19 (0.22 to the contact column
at 12.26; the 11.76 routing track stays inside the pin).

### sg13g2_hv_dllr_1 — 33 sites (15.84 µm)
Same device multiset as dlhr_1, low-enable (pin GATE_N; pass-gate
phases swapped exactly as dllrq_1 vs dlhrq_1).  Layout from the LV
twin; one butted VDD finger removed/restored.  Activ normalisation:
two contact-head slabs' bottoms raised (x3.36..3.795 to 3.05,
x9.705..9.9 to 3.01) and the deep 2.69-channel region x9.9..12.96
translated up 0.10 µm, so the well jog (bottom 2.39) clears the NMOS
halos.  Metal1 edits: run-splitting notches in the wide block at
(7.4..7.9, 1.44) and (8.11, 1.90..2.09), D pin pad narrowed 0.73→0.76
and GATE_N pin pad 2.78→2.74 (0.22 clearances; tracks 1.2 and
1.68/2.16/2.64 stay inside the pins).  Magic reports 4 M1.e (known
non-fatal tool diff; KLayout maximal deck clean).

## Conventions verified by the gate (all four)

* prBoundary 189/4 = (0,0)–(W,7.140), W = k × 0.48;
* Metal1 rails VSS y[-0.22,0.22] / VDD y[6.825,7.360], rail tap
  contacts on the site-centred 0.48 grid (site centres 0.24+0.48k);
* ThickGateOx 44/0 boundary +0.27 in x / +0.42 in y, DigiBnd 16/0 =
  boundary (same as the retargeted library);
* N-well 31/0 strict construction: bottom 2.570 (local jogs ≥0.62
  inside the cell span where flagged above), top 7.530, lateral
  overhang exactly ±0.62, ≥0.62 enclosure of PMOS active, ≥0.62
  spacing to NMOS active and p-tap;
* all gates L = 0.45; contacts 0.16×0.16; pins Metal1.pin 8/2 with
  Metal1.text 8/25 labels, every signal pin crossed by a 0.48 track
  (cell_verify reports zero off-track pins).

## What the gate does not certify

Density (chip-level, skipped with --no_density as for the whole
library), antenna, PEX/post-layout timing (the .lib has no
characterisation data for these four cells — charlib would need to run
before they are used in synthesis), and LEF pin data (`gen_lef.py` has
not been re-run; these cells are not yet merged into
gds/sg13g2_stdcell_hv.gds or the LEF/lib views — merging is a separate,
deliberate library-release step).  Magic's Cnt.c/M1.e interpretation
diffs also flag the shipped library cells and are non-fatal by the
gate's definition; NW.* is fatal and is zero for all four.
